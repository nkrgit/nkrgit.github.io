The job

Check a US credit-card business against business-defined risk thresholds on every run, across 5M+ records, and get each flagged exception to the client team that can fix it.

What I built

The first 75 controls in SQL, SAS and Excel as an Analyst II, then scaled the suite to 275 automated compliance and data-quality controls across the TD Bankcard and Nordstrom portfolios as a Specialist. The controls cover credit line increases and decreases (CLI/CLD), Rewards, SCRA, MLA, Get Help, card activation and account closures.

What changed

The suite has flagged 5,000+ exceptions: regulatory breaches, control breaches, system failures and agent errors, each worked with client teams through to corrective action. Comparing process flows across servicing channels surfaced 20+ gaps, closed before they became regulatory issues.

The Six-Hour Pipeline

The job

Data ingestion took six hours and more than twenty hand-run code steps before any analysis could start.

What I built

Rebuilt the ingestion with SQL macros and subqueries, then turned the 20+ manual runs into a one-click process with built-in QC checks and automated status alerts. Reusable SQL, Python and SAS templates followed, along with SQL transformations feeding BMO Private Banking leadership dashboards.

What changed

Runtime fell 67% (6h → 2h); the templates cut runtime a further 40% and manual effort 30%. Along the way I mentored 3+ analysts on the same stack.

  • SQL
  • Python (pandas)
  • SAS
  • Dashboards
